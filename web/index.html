How to read this page

Risk tiers

Each district gets a tier from how many cases are expected for every 100,000 residents in the next 14 days. Low is under 3, Very high is 16 or more.

Expected cases

A best estimate for the district, shown with a likely range. Small districts swing a lot, so look at the tier and the range, not one exact number.

Estimates, not counts

The Ministry reports cases by state. District figures are shared out from the state total using population, weather and where outbreaks are. They show where to look first.

What actually predicts dengue

Each group of factors is added to the model and scored only on years it has never seen. A factor earns its place by lowering forecast error, not by looking correlated.

When weather shows up in cases

Correlation between a weather anomaly and national cases that many weeks later, after removing season and year.

Rain peaks at 5 to 7 weeks, matching the mosquito life cycle plus incubation. Every link is weak.

Weather effects once recent cases are known

Change in incidence per one standard deviation of weather, one to three months earlier.

Lines crossing 1.0 cannot be told apart from no effect. Warm spells the month before is the clearest signal.
